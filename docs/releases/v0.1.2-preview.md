Solo Camera Coach workflow fix, expanded 62-exercise library, macOS support, and persistence/progression improvements for Samsung Galaxy S22 and Galaxy Tab A9+.

### What changed in 0.1.2 (`versionCode = 3`)

- **Solo-user Camera Coach start**: The **Start tracked set** button is now enabled as soon as the camera opens (`failure == null`), without requiring full-body framing first. Tapping start begins a **5-second countdown** (with optional spoken countdown cues and a **Cancel countdown** button) so a single user can tap the screen, place the device on a stand, and step back into full-body view. Tracking interruptions are only flagged after full-body tracking has been established during the set.
- **Expanded exercise library (62 exercises) & 4 workout variants**: Added 42 new exercises across `Squat`, `Hinge`, `Push`, `Pull`, `Core`, `Accessory`, `Warm-up`, and `Mobility` patterns covering `Bodyweight`, `Dumbbells`, `Bands`, and `Bench`. Workout generation now rotates through **Full Body A, B, C, and D**.
- **Persistence & domain improvements**:
  - Primary snapshot persistence now uses `AtomicFile` (`adhils-fitness-snapshot.json`) with transparent migration from Room to avoid Android's 2 MB SQLite `CursorWindow` limit (`SQLiteBlobTooBigException`).
  - `Training.nextLoad` now tracks the highest working-set weight that met `minReps` rather than `minOf`, so ramping weight across working sets does not lower the next session's target load.
  - `ProfileScreen` syncs on the full `Profile` value, `WorkoutScreen` coerces `setIndex` when an AI Coach proposal reduces planned sets, and `FitnessViewModel.status()` reads `limitUsd` dynamically.
- **macOS build & companion support**: Added `scripts/bootstrap.sh`, `scripts/build.sh`, and macOS Keychain/OpenSSL support in `companion/`.

### Installation note

Download **ADhils-Fitness-0.1.2-preview-arm64.apk** below. Because this APK was built and signed on macOS, its debug signing certificate differs from the earlier Windows-built 0.1.0/0.1.1 previews. If you have existing data on your device from 0.1.0/0.1.1:
1. Open **Settings → Export encrypted backup** before updating.
2. Uninstall the previous Windows-signed preview, then install **ADhils-Fitness-0.1.2-preview-arm64.apk**.
3. Open **Settings → Import backup** to restore your profiles and workout history.
